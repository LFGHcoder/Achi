A simple **Java terminal game** inspired by the traditional **Achi board game** — a 3×3 strategy game where two players place and slide their pieces to align three in a row.  
This project demonstrates **object-oriented programming**, clean console UI, and modular Java design.

---

## 🧠 How to Play

- 👥 Two players take turns placing **three pieces each** on a 3×3 board.  
- ➕ After all pieces are placed, players **slide one piece per turn** along valid lines.  
- 🏆 First player to **align three in a row** wins!

---
